CURRENT RIDE-DERIVED FONT SYSTEM - founder-directed visual redesign, 29 October 2026

The Ride for Parkinson's source requests Inter 400/500/600/700/800 and
Libre Franklin 700/800. Meridian Station now adopts these exact families,
not the earlier Source fonts. Both are distributed under SIL Open Font
License 1.1. Unmodified Latin variable WOFF2 binaries and required copyright/
license notices are bundled locally. No font service is contacted by visitors.
Weights declared: Inter 400-800; Libre Franklin 700-800. Latin coverage includes
the current site's English text, curly punctuation and downward arrow; it is
not a promise of universal script coverage.

Distribution selected using the official Google Fonts CSS:
