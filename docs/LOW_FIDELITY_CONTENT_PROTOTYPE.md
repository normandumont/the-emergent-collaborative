## Current P2. Why look at change over time?

### Draft page copy

## Why look at change over time?

A research finding or an isolated observation can leave practical questions unanswered. What was measured, under what conditions, and what happened before or after? A snapshot can miss context that matters to the question.

We want to make research easier to explore alongside questions about everyday change, without treating personal observations as equivalent evidence. Collecting more information is not the goal in itself: its relevance, quality, missing context, and burden matter.

For us, **longitudinal** means paying attention to change over time rather than treating each observation as an isolated snapshot. It does not mean assuming that one event caused another.

## Current P3. Evidence. Experience. Exploration.

### Draft page copy

## Evidence. Experience. Exploration.

Three connected ideas guide the project. Keeping their differences visible matters as much as exploring them together.

### Evidence

**What does published research report, and how strong is its support?**

Evidence means published research, how it was studied, the sources supporting a claim, and its limitations. We intend to make claims traceable. A citation is a starting point for checking a claim, not a reason to stop asking questions.

### Experience

**What does a person notice over time?**

Experience means what a person observes or records over time, with context and limitations. It helps shape the questions worth asking. We will not treat one person's observation as proof of a treatment effect or a cause.

That does not dismiss lived experience. When personal reports are used in research, we will ask how they were collected and what the study can reasonably conclude.

### Exploration

**What could emerging technology help us investigate?**

Exploration means questions, prototypes, methods, and technologies being investigated, not validation. AI, sensing, and other tools are not answers in themselves. We intend to judge experiments by what they can demonstrate and to state what remains unknown.

An experimental tool is not established medical treatment. People remain responsible for interpretation and decisions.

### Research Assistance

The intended direction is AI-assisted research discovery and explanation, with source-linked outputs, citations, provenance, limitations, and uncertainty visible. Comparing findings should help people ask better questions, not delegate medical answers to an autonomous system.

### Longitudinal Understanding

We are exploring how observations over time could be understood with their context, missingness, provenance, and measurement limitations. Patterns would be questions to investigate, not proof of causation. Any future collection requires a justified purpose and separate safeguards.

### Initiatives

Parkinson's is the first application of this direction. The mission is broader, but there is no promise to expand to other conditions or to build three separate products.

### Review notes

Purpose/message: preserve distinct epistemic roles, then explain connected product
directions. Optional detail is visible prose, not tabs/cards. Classification:
editorial policy and founder intent, not findings. No source entry is needed for
an asserted scientific result because none is made. Next: first initiative.
