\# The Emergent Collaborative — Agent Instructions



Before significant product, UX, architecture, or implementation work:



1\. Read docs/GENESIS.md.

2\. Preserve the distinction between Evidence, Experience, and Exploration.

3\. Never present personal observations as scientific evidence.

4\. Never present experimental technology as established medical treatment.

5\. Preserve citations and provenance for scientific information.

6\. Parkinson's is the first initiative, not the platform boundary.

7\. Design for longitudinal data rather than isolated snapshots.

8\. Keep public research data and private personal data architecturally separate.

9\. Avoid unnecessary coupling to one AI vendor, database, or cloud.

10\. Do not implement features until requirements and acceptance criteria exist.



The founder is the final decision maker.



Prefer simple, extensible architectures over premature complexity.
